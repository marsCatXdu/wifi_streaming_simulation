# Score-aware temporal-T2 V2 engineering victory against STR MLO

This directory is the compact, version-controlled snapshot of the first
selective-duplication candidate that passes every frozen engineering gate
against STR MLO in the unchanged neutral mixed-4x4 environment.  It uses the
score-aware emergency admission policy with the primary predictor, score
threshold, P-frame gate, action, 0.6% refill, 10-second bucket, and
conservative airtime reservation otherwise unchanged from V1.

The campaign used seeds `1251` through `1298`, ns-3 run `1`, and one policy/STR
pair per seed.  All 96 raw runs from project commit `eb7f960` passed the
manifest-pinned strict validator.  Reserved final-confirmation seeds `1301`
through `1348` were not used.  This is engineering evidence, not final
confirmation.

## Qualification result

| Metric | Score-aware T2 V2 | STR MLO | Paired comparison | Gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| All-generated deadline-miss rate | 0.5729% (495/86,400) | 0.7998% (691/86,400) | policy-minus-STR 95% interval `[-0.3194, -0.1377]` percentage points | Pass |
| Mean per-run completed-frame HF7 P99 | 17.192 ms | 18.875 ms | policy-minus-STR 95% interval `[-2.643, -0.795]` ms | Pass |
| Sender PHY airtime ratio | - | - | 1.1217, 95% interval `[1.0847, 1.1565]` | Pass (`< 1.20`) |
| Background-throughput loss | - | - | 0.0054%, 95% interval `[0.0027%, 0.0081%]` | Pass (`<= 1%`) |

The policy reduces misses by 28.36% relative to its matched STR baseline and
lowers mean per-run completed P99 by 1.682 ms, or 8.91%.  The miss comparison
favors the policy in 33 pairs, ties in 5, and favors STR in 10.  The P99
comparison favors the policy in 31 of 48 pairs.  Both performance metrics
favor the policy in 23 pairs; both favor STR in 5.

Completed-frame latency always appears beside the all-generated miss rate.
Late completions remain in the P99 population; incomplete frames remain in
the miss denominator.  The completion CDF/PDF figures are descriptive and do
not replace the paired mean-of-per-run-P99 qualification estimand.

## What V2 established

V2 made 4,944 actions.  Reconstructing the primary-copy outcome gives 1,232
misses before duplication and 495 final union misses.  Of 771 acted primary
misses, 737 were rescued, for 95.59% rescue efficiency.  The mechanism is
therefore still highly effective once a frame is admitted.

The emergency tier corrected V1's admission inversion:

| Population | Frames | Primary-copy misses | Primary-miss risk |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All score-threshold passers | 7,484 | 933 | 12.47% |
| Admitted actions | 4,944 | 771 | 15.59% |
| Guard rejected | 2,540 | 162 | 6.38% |

The 2,116 emergency admissions are especially concentrated: they contain 453
primary misses (21.41%), while the 2,828 strict admissions contain 318
(11.24%).  V1 had rejected candidates with higher primary risk than admitted
ones; V2 has the desired ordering.  Because V1 and V2 used different
engineering seeds, their aggregate performance difference is not itself a
paired causal estimate, but the within-V2 admission strata directly confirm
that the new guard spends its scarce actions on higher-risk candidates.

The factual action outcomes are 3,128 accelerated on-time primaries, 1,045
on-time primaries with no completion-time benefit, 737 rescued primary
misses, and 34 late or incomplete union outcomes.  Accelerated and no-benefit
actions still consume 54.28% and 29.13% of measured action airtime,
respectively.  Better treated-outcome and secondary-cost ranking therefore
remain worthwhile even though V2 passes.

The burst result also improves in absolute terms.  V2 has 427 miss episodes,
including 39 multi-frame episodes containing 107 misses, with maximum length
6.  STR has 571 episodes, including 71 multi-frame episodes containing 191
misses, with maximum length 11.  This is not merely survivor-conditioned tail
improvement or replacement of rare long bursts by many short ones.

## Remaining misses and next controlled question

The 495 final misses decompose exactly as follows:

| Terminal decision status | Final misses |
| --- | ---: |
| Below score threshold | 170 |
| Airtime guard rejected | 162 |
| History warmup | 72 |
| I-frame restricted | 48 |
| Admitted action still late/incomplete | 34 |
| Outside decision window | 9 |

Admission still has measurable headroom.  Mean measured secondary airtime is
240.43 ms/run.  All 7,484 threshold passers carry 355.25 ms/run of learned
predicted cost, below the 360 ms/run generated by the 0.6% refill over a
60-second experiment; rejected candidates account for 120.84 ms/run of that
prediction.  If every rejected candidate retained the current rescue rate,
the optimistic miss point would be about 0.394%.  This is a sensitivity
projection, not a closed-loop result: extra copies change contention, actual
cost, and later observations.

The next isolated admission experiment should therefore keep the frozen
predictor, score, action, emergency tier, and refill while increasing causal
credit carry-over from 10 seconds to the full 60-second horizon.  It should be
developed only on already-open engineering seeds.  Only a closed-loop result
can decide whether unused average budget can be converted into additional
rescues while keeping the airtime interval below 1.20.

After that isolation, the two most concrete model/policy targets are the 170
below-threshold misses and startup.  The first eight frames per run account
for 72 misses among only 384 frames (18.75% risk) because temporal history is
unavailable.  The study must explicitly choose startup or steady-state
semantics; a causal non-temporal startup fallback or equal pre-roll across all
treatments is preferable to silently excluding those frames.  The P-only gate
leaves another 48 I-frame misses, so any extension should be I-frame-specific
rather than automatic duplication of every large I-frame.

Do not consume final-confirmation seeds just because this V2 result passes.
The current result is a strong engineering milestone but is still short of
the longer-term aspiration of more than 50% fewer misses than STR.  Freeze the
final candidate and its analyzer before opening seeds `1301` through `1348`.
